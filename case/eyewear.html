Case study · 2026

Glasses fitted from a live photo

People can get measured for glasses and see frames that suit them from a live photo, with no clinic visit and no card held to the face. I trained the face-shape model behind the frame recommendations and added the gaze tracking that keeps the measurement steady.

At a glance

  • Client: an eyewear business
  • My role: trained the face-shape model, added gaze tracking, and deployed the service on Google Cloud. With my team, built the pupillary distance measurement.
  • Result: face-shape F1 from 74 to 86, on a balanced test set
  • Stack: PyTorch, DenseNet-121, MediaPipe, FastAPI, Google Cloud, CI/CD

The problem

Glasses need a measurement of the distance between the pupils. That usually means a visit to a clinic or pharmacy. Online tools ask people to hold a credit card to their face for scale, which is awkward and puts many of them off. Choosing frames online is guesswork too.

What I built and decided

  • Measurement with no reference object. Our team measured pupillary distance from one live image, with nothing held to the face.
  • Gaze tracking first. A measurement is only as good as the photo. I added gaze tracking, so the system checks that the person is well placed and looking at the camera before it measures.
  • A face-shape model from uneven data. The images came from two sources, one balanced and one heavily skewed, where the rarest shape had about one image for every eight of the most common. I cropped the faces, augmented every class, and cleaned labels where neighbouring shapes such as oval and oblong were easy to confuse. A class-weighted focal loss then gave rare shapes more weight and focused training on the hard cases. The DenseNet-121 classifier went from F1 74 to 86.
  • Recommendations from several features. Pretrained models estimate age range, gender and skin tone. Together with face shape, they decide which frames are suggested.
  • Shipped on Google Cloud. I deployed the service through a CI/CD pipeline and configured the load balancer in front of it.
