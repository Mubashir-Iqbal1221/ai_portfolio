Beyond ONE: from a news stream to alerts you can trace

I designed the alert pipeline of a news-intelligence product. A relevancy gate drops what does not matter, then claims are extracted, scored and qualified into alerts that cite their sources.

What I did

  • Put an embedding gate first (threshold 0.6), so irrelevant articles never reach the costly steps.
  • Rewrote the claim-relations prompt: correct pair decisions went from 73% to 100% on a 40-pair synthetic test, and malformed responses from 5 requests to 0.
  • Planned the tickets and reviewed PRs for two engineers, with the team lead's agreement.

How the gate scored

Gate: relevantGate: not relevant
Labelled relevant8311
Labelled not relevant1226

F1 0.88 on 132 labelled events.

Worth flagging

  • 72 of the 132 events are synthetic or reconstructed, and the labels are proposals, not confirmed truth.
  • Accuracy is 0.75 for the Middle East and Africa against 0.91 for Latin America. Most misses score just under the 0.6 threshold.
  • The 100% prompt result is a small test I labelled myself.
