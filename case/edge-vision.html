VISTOS: finding events on camera streams, on one Jetson

VISTOS spots falls and fights on camera streams and runs on an NVIDIA Jetson. I worked on the Jetson and DeepStream pipeline and trained its vision model.

What it does

  • Filters early: frames with no people are skipped before the costly steps.
  • Flags falls and fights from body overlap and pose-landmark movement.
  • An event engine removes repeats and saves clips for operators.

How it ran

1 stream, 8 fps0.361, limited
2 streams, 6 fps0.470, limited
3 streams, 4 fps0.650, limited
4 streams, 2 fps0.889, stable
8 streams, 1 fps0.889, stable

Benchmark on the repository's runner: 20 s warm-up, 45 s measured, 2 repeats.

Worth flagging

  • This measures throughput only. How often events are detected correctly is not reported here.
  • Stable runs top out at 0.889, so "stable" is the pass mark, not a ratio of 1.0.
