About

I am a machine learning engineer at RedBuffer in Islamabad, working on two kinds of AI: LLM agents that use real tools, and vision models that work from live camera feeds. With agents, one that fails loudly is more useful to me than one that hides its mistakes behind confident answers.

That work has spanned news intelligence, surveillance and eyewear retail, from training models on small datasets to leading the design of whole services. Since starting my career there in August 2024, I have been promoted to mid-level.

Before this came two internships in NLP and ML engineering, almost a year in math education, and degrees in mathematics and data science.
